Oliver Wendell Holmes Sr.

Born 1809 Died 1894 American American Romanticism

Biography

Oliver Wendell Holmes Sr. was born on August 29, 1809, in Cambridge, Massachusetts, and became a physician, poet, and essayist, one of the central figures of the New England literary establishment. His poem Old Ironsides (1830), written at twenty-one, caused such public response that it saved the USS Constitution from demolition. His Autocrat of the Breakfast-Table (1858) made him one of the most widely read American writers of his era. He died in Boston in 1894.
