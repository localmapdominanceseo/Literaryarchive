James Russell Lowell

Born 1819 Died 1891 American American Romanticism

Biography

James Russell Lowell was born on February 22, 1819, in Cambridge, Massachusetts, and became a poet, critic, essayist, and diplomat, one of the central figures of the New England literary establishment. He was the first editor of The Atlantic Monthly and later served as U.S. Ambassador to Spain and Britain. He died in Cambridge in 1891.
