Mary E. Wilkins Freeman

Born 1852 Died 1930 American American Realism

Biography

Mary Eleanor Wilkins Freeman was born on October 31, 1852, in Randolph, Massachusetts. One of the leading figures of New England regional realism, she wrote stories of fierce, independent women constrained by poverty and social convention. 'The Revolt of Mother' (1890) is her most celebrated story. She died in Metuchen, New Jersey, in 1930.
