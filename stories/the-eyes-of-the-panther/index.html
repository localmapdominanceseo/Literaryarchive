The Eyes of the Panther

by Ambrose Bierce

Written 1800 Published 1800 Public Domain

Published in 1897 in Can Such Things Be?, 'The Eyes of the Panther' by Ambrose Bierce describes a woman who was frightened by a panther while in the womb. She sees her reflection as a panther, not a human face. She refuses her lover's proposal. He finds her dead, and a panther is shot nearby.

About Ambrose Bierce

Ambrose Bierce was born on June 24, 1842, in Horse Cave Creek, Ohio. A Civil War veteran, he became one of the most mordant American journalists and fiction writers of the Gilded Age, best known for his Civil War stories and The Devil's Dictionary. He disappeared in Mexico in 1913 or 1914 under unknown circumstances.
