The Boarding House

by James Joyce

Written 1800 Published 1800 Public Domain

Published in 1914 in Dubliners, 'The Boarding House' follows Mrs. Mooney, a boarding house keeper who has arranged — through strategic inaction — for her daughter Polly and the clerk Mr. Doran to become involved. She then calls Mr. Doran to account and forces a marriage proposal from him.

Themes

The social machinery that constrains individual lives — nobody makes an explicit demand; the pressure is entirely social and economic, and it is completely effective.

Historical Context

Published in Dubliners (1914). Part of the 'adolescence' section.

About James Joyce

James Joyce was born on February 2, 1882, in Dublin, Ireland. His works — Dubliners (1914), A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man (1916), Ulysses (1922), and Finnegans Wake (1939) — redefined what the novel could be. Ulysses is widely considered the greatest novel of the twentieth century. He died in Zurich in 1941.
