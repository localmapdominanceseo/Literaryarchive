Persian Classical Poetry

Persian classical poetry, spanning roughly the 9th through 15th centuries CE, produced some of the most celebrated literary works in the Islamic world, including the Rubáiyát of Omar Khayyám, the Masnavi of Rumi, and the Divan of Hafiz. Persian poetry is known for its elaborate imagery, philosophical depth, and the ambiguity between mystical and earthly love.
