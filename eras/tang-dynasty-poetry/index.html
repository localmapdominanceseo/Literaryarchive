Tang Dynasty Poetry

The Tang Dynasty (618-907 CE) is considered the golden age of Chinese poetry, producing Du Fu, Li Bai, Wang Wei, and hundreds of other major poets. Tang poetry ranged from the formal regulated verse of Du Fu to the lyrical freedom of Li Bai, and established the aesthetic standards that Chinese poets would measure themselves against for the next thousand years.
